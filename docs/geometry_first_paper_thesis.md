# Geometry-first paper thesis

## Central claim

This manuscript is not primarily another effective scalar cosmology.

Its primary claim is methodological:

> Physical degrees of freedom should be identified after action variation,
> constraints, boundary conditions, gauge reduction, and physical-Hessian
> reduction. Effective fields may then be used as response coordinates or
> compatibility languages, but are not automatically the fundamental ontology.

## Core workflow

geometry
-> action
-> constraints and boundary conditions
-> gauge quotient
-> physical tangent space
-> physical Hessian
-> Schur/Feshbach response
-> metric/matter response
-> optical propagation
-> observer-frame observables.

## Role of EFT/QFT

The paper does not claim EFT or QFT are generally invalid.

It uses the curvature-aware effective-representation defect as a concrete
example showing that an effective representation can be useful yet fail to be
globally equivalent to the full constrained geometry.

The foundational BHSM cosmology transport remains the action-reduced physical
n=2 system. Horndeski/EFT variables are compatibility and translation tools.

## Why the failures stay in the paper

Rejected homogeneous and foreground proxy models demonstrate the workflow:

hypothesis -> freeze -> test -> reject -> move upstream.

They must not be hidden or retuned away.

## Main predictive examples

- physical n=2 scalar carrier after exceptional-mode audit;
- one SN scalar calibration has rank one on a two-dimensional state;
- common one-mode angular axis across linear observables;
- gauge-invariant closed-S3 luminosity-distance kernel;
- stochastic optical state/covariance propagation;
- rank(C_topo) <= m for m physical texture channels.

## Claim boundary

The microscopic local source normalization and normalized compact
full-preimage background remain open. The paper must keep those upstream
unknowns explicit.
