## Referee-critical caveats
- In a common closed-FLRW convention, our `n=1` scalar level maps to literature `N=2`, where standard gauge-invariant scalar-metric constructions contain exceptional/singular factors such as `1/(N^2-4K)` for `K=+1`.

- `D_kin = alpha_K + 3 alpha_B^2/2 > 0` is retained as an inherited cubic-Galileon kinetic diagnostic. Bellini-Sawicki's standard perturbation formulation assumes spatial flatness, so this quantity alone is not a complete closed-FLRW stability proof.
