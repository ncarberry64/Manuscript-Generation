## Cosmology consequence

The physical n=2 topographic carrier is represented first as an action-owned
envelope/boundary mode on the physical tangent space.  The two-dimensional
phase-space pair (q2,Pi2) is generated by the reduced action.  A Horndeski or
Stueckelberg variable may represent its effective four-dimensional response
but is not counted as an additional ontological scalar.

The curved-EFT DAE remains a compatibility audit.  It is not part of the
foundational mode-count theorem on this branch.
