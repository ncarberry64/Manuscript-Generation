# Conditional corrected-group calculation

This new calculation uses the delivered corrected 66×66 field stamp. It does not repeat native pipeline processing, fit quasars, open another filter, change a lens prediction, or alter the physical guard.

The applied mean is `a[p,segment] + m[j] f[p] + kappa v[j]/2 L(f²)[p]`. Here `m=mean(t)` and `v=mean(t²)` use the actual two-read group supports. `L` is the nearest-neighbour, zero-exterior-flux graph Laplacian on all 66×66 pixels. Antisymmetric edge transport preserves total charge before pixel-specific ramp selection; mixed-history fitted slopes need not preserve a total rate. This leading time-dependent mean is a declared conditional hypothesis, not an identified NIRCam charge-migration calibration.

All 289 rates inside the 17×17 ROI are first fit jointly with positive kappa and 306 segment intercepts. Intercepts are profiled by GLS; JUMP starts a new intercept and DNU/SAT groups are excluded. Context rates are fixed from groups zero through seven only. A second application replaces the free ROI rates with `flat/PHOTMJSR * (F P(cx,cy)+b0+bx dx+by dy)` using the delivered dated, prescribed-4000 K STPSF. This evaluates all six optical nuisance parameters jointly with kappa, including every derivative through `L(f²)`.

The ninth group is excluded from both fitting and its covariance pilot for the 257 full-nine-group, no-jump wing pixels. The forecast includes `C_ht C_tt^-1 (y_t-mu_t)` and the conditional covariance, together with the full fitted-parameter and segment-intercept uncertainty. It is an **internal temporal holdout on previously consumed field data**, not a historically untouched or independent observation.

Covariance is frozen from training data: ideal Poisson shared reads plus CDS read noise. Exact native nine-group nonlinearity Jacobians, shared SIRS spatial/reference covariance, dark/calibration covariance, and padding-rate uncertainty remain unresolved. The measured native groups are nonlinearity-corrected averages; correction of a read average is not the average of independently corrected reads. Fixed observed saturation/jump selection also conditions the calculation. No residual rescaling is applied. Reported standard errors and p values are local, conditional diagnostics, not physically calibrated probabilities.

The free-rate history model improves ninth-group raw RMS from 10.20884 to 9.67245 DN. The coupled fixed-optical-PSF model instead worsens held RMS from 25.23791 to 25.58811 DN and held Q from 1542.413 to 1573.909 on 257 pixels. Thus the temporal term cannot by itself repair the optical response. Tight curvature errors on kappa under this failed mean do not identify a physical detector coefficient.

`results.json` records the exact input/source hashes, equations, all four fits, scales and limits. Each `*_arrays.npz` stores the actual fitted rates, group residuals, joint profile covariance, singular values, and correlated held forecasts/covariance. Its record-column schema and parameter coordinates are in `results.json`. All physical-calibration and lens-validation flags remain false. Reproduce with `scripts/run_he0435_read_history.py`; no FITS SCI arrays outside the delivered consumed field stamp are read (the original CAL supplies only its SCI-header PHOTMJSR).
