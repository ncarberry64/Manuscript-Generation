\begin{abstract}
We develop a conditional cosmological response chain on a closed $S^3$
parent, resolving constraints and gauge redundancy before assigning physical
degrees of freedom. The regular one-scalar quotient removes the exceptional
$n=1$ sector; the first ordinary physical scalar carrier is $n=2$.
One selected normalized profile has a two-component phase-space state,
while its observer-sky dipole is exactly proportional to
$\sin(2\psi)\hat{\mathbf n}\cdot\hat{\mathbf p}$.
Schur/Feshbach reduction supplies the induced metric response, shared
by growth, Weyl/lensing and luminosity-distance kernels at fixed observed
redshift. Jacobi/Sachs propagation provides a seven-state optical system
and transport equations for its mean and covariance. For $m$ coherent
global amplitudes and fixed response, the isolated topographic contribution
satisfies $\operatorname{rank}C_{\rm topo}\le m$; a local noise field alone
imposes no such channel count. Two prospective rejection gates test a
shared deterministic state and the minimal coherent one-amplitude covariance,
each at $p<0.01$ under a preregistered survey design.
A frozen retrospective interpretation study reconstructs source diagnostics
for 3,521 supernova rows and adds independent foreground information.
With $\Delta\chi^2=\chi^2_{\rm frozen}-\chi^2_{\rm null}$, conditional held-out
Pantheon+ source/environment adjustment gives $-0.883$, whereas DES transfer
gives $+1.321$. Fixing an external peculiar-velocity mean at coefficient one
gives $-4.909$ for Pantheon+, but incomplete joint uncertainty propagation
makes this sensitivity only. Aggregate shell tests do not localize residual
correlations. The current data neither establish a coherent topographic
signal nor reject the conditional framework. Absolute source normalization,
complete matter--radiation stability, the independently selected state
direction and joint observational covariance remain open. No scientific
parameter is retuned to improve these results.
\end{abstract}
