\begin{abstract}
We develop a geometry-first cosmological workflow in which physical degrees of
freedom are identified after constraints, boundary conditions, gauge
redundancies and the physical Hessian are resolved. Within a conditional
Berger--Hopf cosmological bridge on a closed hyperspherical parent, a
selected physical $n=2$ harmonic carries one two-component phase-space state.
Its induced metric response supplies reference growth and Weyl kernels and
a gauge-invariant luminosity-distance map at fixed observed redshift.
Jacobi/Sachs equations propagate photon redshift, convergence, shear and
their stochastic covariance. For a fixed response to $m$ coherent random
texture amplitudes, the isolated topographic covariance has rank at most
$m$; one local noise type alone does not imply rank one.
We formulate two prospective rejection gates at $p<0.01$: consistency of
deterministic observables with one shared state, and consistency of isolated
covariance with the minimal one-amplitude realization. Survey selection,
nuisance treatment and finite-sample calibration must be frozen before
target inspection. Homogeneous localization and three foreground-transfer
candidates fail their retained tests. A curvature-dependent defect in one
effective representation is reported without generalizing it to EFT or QFT.
Complete compact-background normalization, action-owned local couplings and
source statistics remain open. The contribution is a reproducible,
conditional response chain and explicit falsification criteria, rather than
a completed microscopic cosmology.
\end{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} cosmology; hyperspherical geometry; constrained dynamics; optical scalar equations; luminosity distance; weak lensing; stochastic geometry; falsifiable cosmology
