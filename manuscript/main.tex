\begin{abstract}
We develop a geometry-first cosmological workflow in which physical degrees of
freedom are identified only after action variation, constraint enforcement,
gauge reduction, and physical-Hessian reduction, rather than assumed as
fundamental fields. Applied to a closed hyperspherical parent geometry
$S^3(R_H)$, the construction isolates a physical $n=2$ reduced phase-space
state and induces its metric response through the same constrained Hessian.
The state is propagated to observations using a gauge-invariant closed-$S^3$
luminosity-distance kernel and Jacobi/Sachs optical transport for photon
redshift, convergence, and shear. Unresolved local geometry is represented
statistically, yielding the structural prediction
$\operatorname{rank}C_{\rm topo}\le m$ for $m$ independent physical
topographic texture channels; the one-channel limit is rank one.
Effective-field/Horndeski variables are retained as compatibility languages
rather than as the defining ontology. A curvature-dependent
constraint-preservation defect in one effective representation, together with
rejected preregistered foreground proxies, illustrates the distinction between
representation and physical reduction. The framework produces falsifiable
multi-observable tests while leaving the upstream action-owned source
normalization explicitly open.
\end{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} cosmology; hyperspherical geometry; constrained dynamics; optical scalar equations; luminosity distance; weak lensing; stochastic geometry; falsifiable cosmology
