\subsection{Retained action, fields and conditional data}
We now evaluate a local encounter, rather than identify its missing inputs.
The retained four-dimensional action is Einstein gravity plus ordinary
perfect-fluid matter and the recovered physical Path--B target sector,
\begin{align}
 S_{\rm loc}&=\frac{c^4}{16\pi G}\int\dd t\dd^3x\,N\sqrt h\,R
       +S_{\rm fluid}+S_{\rm GHY}+S_\eta+S_{\rm YM},\\

 F(X)&=\frac{\kappa_\eta}{2}X+\frac18X^4,

 \label{eq:sl-local-action}
\end{align}
The target is $S^6\subset\R\oplus\mathbb C^3$, with
$\eta=(s,z)$, $s^2+2z^\dagger z=1$ and target norm
$\dd s^2+2\dd z^\dagger\dd z$. The connection is $SU(3)$ on $z$;
$N$ is the physical static lapse and $h$ the spatial metric.
Here $\kappa_\eta$ denotes the original target coefficient $\kappa_1$,
distinguished from the gravitational coefficient. We require
$w_0\kappa_\eta>0$; its dimensional value remains a specified coupling,
not a lens fit. The higher-power coefficient and the dimensions of $X$
are those of the original target-sector units.
The intrinsic boundary action contains $C_\partial R_4$; setting
$C_\partial=c^4/(16\pi G)$ defines the retained effective gravity sector.
This does not derive the cap reduction or its reaction terms.
We condition on no additional local cap traction or vacuum energy in this
sector. Restoring a nonzero cap reaction requires its own field equation.
The local isolated solution is inserted in the declared weak-field
comparison with cosmological propagation. Pure background-curvature

\subsection{Exact common-domain physical Hessian and source}

$r_0<\varrho<L$, with
\begin{equation}

\end{equation}

\begin{equation}

\end{equation}
These are physical log lapse and spatial conformal potential, equal to

Use the same real Dirichlet profile in all three columns,
\begin{equation}

       \sin\frac{\pi(\varrho-r_0)}\ell,
