\section{Frozen 2MRS foreground-transfer test}
\label{sec:2mrs-foreground-transfer}

The illustrative local-coherence cutoff is replaced here by a foreground
field constructed from the 2MRS galaxy distribution.  For supernova \(i\),
\[
{\cal F}_i =
{C_K(\chi_i)\over S_K(\chi_i)}
\sum_s
\Delta\chi_{is}\,
\delta_s(\hat{\mathbf n}_i),
\]
where \(\delta_s\) is the fixed \(10^\circ\) Gaussian-smoothed density contrast
in shell \(s\) and \(\Delta\chi_{is}\) is only the portion of that shell lying
in front of the source.  The score is standardized without using supernova
residuals.  Only one foreground amplitude is fitted.

The frozen analysis used 277 Pantheon+ Hubble-flow
supernovae and 38600 2MRS galaxies with
\(z<0.05\).  Relative to the fixed-axis plus nuisance-control model, adding
the foreground score gave
\[
\Delta\chi^2 = 1.619359,
\qquad
\beta_F = 0.015286
\pm 0.007846\ {\rm mag},
\]
with a redshift-blocked permutation value
\[
p_{\rm perm}=0.076.
\]
The preregistered classification is
\texttt{not\_established}.

As a transfer check, the foreground coefficient was calibrated only for
\(0.01\le z\le0.03\) and then held fixed for \(0.03<z\le0.15\).  The held-out
change was
\[
\Delta\chi^2_{\rm heldout}
=
-4.107958.
\]

This test does not identify the physical source of the foreground signal.
The 2MRS field is a matter-density proxy, not yet an action-normalized BHSM
de-encapsulation map, and conventional peculiar-velocity, lensing and survey
selection effects remain alternative explanations.
