\section{Introduction}
Cosmological models must connect a physical state to several observations
without adding new freedoms at each interface. This paper develops that
requirement as a geometry-first workflow: specify a geometric action,
resolve constraints, boundary conditions and gauge redundancy, reduce the
physical Hessian, and propagate the retained response to matter and light.

The Berger--Hopf Standard Model (BHSM) program motivates this ordering.
Neither constrained perturbation theory nor Schur reduction is new.
Our contribution is their organization into an explicit closed-$S^3$
cosmological response chain, a calibration-rank audit, retained failed
mechanisms, and two prospective rejection gates. Covariant EFT can also
respect constraints and gauge symmetry; this workflow does not distinguish
BHSM from every effective theory.

The regular one-scalar closed-FLRW quotient removes the exceptional $n=1$
scalar sector. The first ordinary scalar carrier is $n=2$ in our convention.
One selected normalized harmonic carries $X_2=(q_2,\Pi_2)^T$; the entire
$n=2$ eigenspace is not two dimensional. Its profile and axis must be fixed
separately by theory or independent calibration. Identification with the
retained BHSM sector remains conditional.

The downstream results are a constrained metric response, reference
growth/Weyl and fixed-observed-redshift distance kernels, and Jacobi/Sachs
propagation of optical means and covariances. Section~\ref{sec:make-or-break}
asks whether deterministic measurements admit one common two-component
state and whether isolated covariance admits one coherent texture amplitude,
with separate prospective $p<0.01$ rejection gates.

This manuscript substantially expands and reframes the prior
Preprints.org study \citep{Carberry2026Topographic}. The physical carrier,
gauge audit, optical propagation and explicit negative results develop and
correct that earlier proposal. We do not claim a resolution of the Hubble
tension, a general failure of EFT/QFT, or an ab initio derivation of every
absolute cosmological amplitude.
