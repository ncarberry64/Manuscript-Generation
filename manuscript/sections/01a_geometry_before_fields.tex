\section{Geometry before fields: the methodological thesis}
\label{sec:geometry-before-fields}

The principal claim of this work is methodological before it is
phenomenological.  An effective field representation may be mathematically
useful without uniquely identifying the underlying physical degrees of
freedom.  We therefore reverse the usual order of construction.

A representation-first workflow has the schematic form
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\text{choose fields}
\rightarrow
\text{write an effective action}
\rightarrow
\text{derive equations}
\rightarrow
\text{fit parameters}
\rightarrow
\text{compare with data}.
}
\label{eq:representation-first}
\end{equation}
The workflow pursued here is instead
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\text{geometry}
\rightarrow
\text{action}
\rightarrow
\text{constraints and boundary conditions}
\rightarrow
\text{gauge quotient}
\rightarrow
\text{physical tangent space}
\rightarrow
\text{physical Hessian}
\rightarrow
\text{reduced response}
\rightarrow
\text{propagation}
\rightarrow
\text{observables}.
}
\label{eq:geometry-first}
\end{equation}

The distinction is operational, not semantic.  In
Eq.~\eqref{eq:geometry-first}, the physical degrees of freedom are not decided
before the constraint problem is solved.  A scalar, vector, or other
field-like variable that appears after reduction is therefore a coordinate on
a surviving physical response unless an additional theorem establishes a
more fundamental interpretation.

\subsection{BHSM as a reduction program}

BHSM implements this logic by constructing the constrained physical tangent
space of a common variational problem and evaluating the action Hessian only
after gauge and constraint reduction.  If the physical variables are divided
into a selected branch and its physical complement, the exact reduced
response is obtained by the Schur/Feshbach map
\begin{equation}
\mathcal K_{\rm phys}
=
A-BK^{-1}B^\dagger.
\end{equation}
This identity is ordinary mathematics; its role here is to prevent
nonphysical or redundant coordinates from being promoted to independent
degrees of freedom.

At the cosmological scale the surviving physical topographic carrier is
represented by the reduced phase-space state
\begin{equation}
X_2=(q_2,\Pi_2)^T,
\end{equation}
while the metric response is induced by the same physical Hessian,
\begin{equation}
h_2
=
-\mathcal A_g^{-1}\mathcal B_{g2}X_2.
\end{equation}
The metric perturbation therefore carries no independent initial datum beyond
the already retained physical mode.

\subsection{Why cosmology is a useful stress test}

Cosmology forces the construction to cross several logically distinct layers:
compact geometry, harmonic selection, constraint reduction, metric response,
matter response, photon propagation, and observer-frame projection.  A
representation that merely reproduces one layer is not automatically
equivalent to the complete chain.

For this reason, the cosmological calculation serves as a stress test of the
geometry-first methodology.  The paper does not ask only whether an effective
equation can be written.  It asks whether one physical reduced state can be
carried, without adding hidden degrees of freedom, from the action to
observable quantities such as luminosity distance, growth, lensing, redshift
perturbations, and their joint covariance.

\subsection{Effective descriptions retain an important role}

Nothing in this construction implies that effective field theory or quantum
field theory is generally invalid.  Effective descriptions are among the most
successful tools in modern physics.  The narrower point is that a useful
effective representation should not automatically be identified with the
fundamental ontology of the system.

Accordingly, effective scalar/Horndeski variables used in this work are
retained as compatibility and translation languages.  The defining transport
law is the constraint-reduced physical response.  Where an effective
representation fails a curvature-dependent constraint-preservation audit,
that failure is treated as evidence about the representation or its domain of
validity rather than as permission to retune the underlying physical mode.
