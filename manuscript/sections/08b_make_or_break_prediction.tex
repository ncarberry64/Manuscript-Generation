\section{Make-or-break prediction: two prospective gates}
\label{sec:make-or-break}
The structural protocol is frozen in
\path{preregistration/bhsm_geometry_first_make_or_break_v1.json}.
No decisive target data have been evaluated under it. Target releases,
masks, bins, covariance and nuisance treatment must be frozen in a dated,
hashed design before unblinding. Existing Pantheon+ and 2MRS analyses are
development/calibration evidence, not new independent validation.

\subsection{Gate A: one shared deterministic state}
For $N$ independent measured combinations and fixed two-column kernels,
\begin{equation}
d=FX_2+\epsilon,\qquad
\chi^2_\perp=\min_X(d-FX)^T\Sigma^{-1}(d-FX).
\end{equation}
The conventional mean and source response are specified in advance.
With rank-$2$ $F$, known positive-definite Gaussian $\Sigma$ and no fitted
nuisances, $\chi^2_\perp\sim\chi^2_{N-2}$. Thus $N>2$ overidentifies the state.
Cholesky whitening and least squares avoid forming $\Sigma^{-1}$.
Reject the specific two-state deterministic realization at $p_A<0.01$.

Both state coordinates vary once across the stacked observations in this
conservative consistency test. This does not create a second calibration
of an absolute prediction. A theory-selected direction would permit a
separately declared one-parameter test. Fitted nuisances, estimated covariance,
data-selected axes and nonlinear kernels invalidate the simple $N-2$
reference law unless incorporated in the predeclared likelihood/simulation.

Independent states or amplitudes per observable, increasing the state
dimension, dropping bad bins and changing kernels after inspection are
forbidden. The carried-forward equatorial axis is
$(211.48^\circ,-12.81^\circ)$; its frame and selection treatment belong in
the target design.

\subsection{Gate B: one coherent stochastic texture amplitude}
For the minimal separable response $O=m_Oq$,
\begin{equation}
C_{\rm topo}=\operatorname{Var}(q)m_Om_O^\dagger,\qquad
\operatorname{rank}C_{\rm topo}\le1.
\end{equation}
Rank equals one only for nonzero variance and response. After independently
specified standard and measurement-noise treatment, report all signed
eigenvalues, $\rho_{21}=\lambda_2/\lambda_1$ when $\lambda_1>0$, and
$C_{ii}C_{jj}-|C_{ij}|^2$.
Observable unit scales are fixed before inspection: exact rank is invariant
under invertible scaling, eigenvalue ratios are not.

The primary statistic is the second-largest eigenvalue of the unprojected
residual covariance in fixed dimensionless units, with an upper-tail
finite-sample calibration. Reject the minimal one-channel stochastic
realization at $p_B<0.01$. Ratios/minors are diagnostics, not extra
uncorrected rejection tests. Negative residual eigenvalues remain visible;
they may reflect subtraction uncertainty or invalid nuisance modeling.

The executable benchmark uses independent Gaussian samples, an independently
fixed total null covariance and 9999 Monte Carlo replicates:
$p=(1+\#\{T_{\rm sim}\ge T_{\rm obs}\})/10000$.
This validates a restricted fixed null, not the composite family of
unknown rank-one covariances. A survey must propagate covariance/loading
estimation, selection and sky correlations through its predeclared null
simulations, with coverage and power checks.

Increasing $m$, deleting observables, fitting conventional covariance to
enforce low rank, or PSD clipping/projection to manufacture rank one are
forbidden. The earlier optical protocol and clipping helper remain historical
provenance; this decisive test does not use the helper.
A higher-channel model needs an independent action derivation frozen before
decisive target inspection and cannot rescue this $m=1$ realization.

\subsection{Separate hypotheses and readiness}
The gates are logically distinct; statistical independence is not assumed
when data overlap. Each uses a $1\%$ threshold. Under both nulls, the
probability of at least one false rejection is bounded by $2\%$, not $1\%$.
Report both gates and shared-data covariance.

Unknown absolute normalization does not prevent stating these structural
restrictions, but applying them requires fixed kernels and a validated
nuisance model. One local noise type does not imply one global amplitude.
The rank-one test is conditional on the coherent finite-amplitude
construction in Section~\ref{sec:bhsm-optical-low-rank}.
