\section{Selective Harmonic Response}

Let
\[
L_c(a)=aR_H\ell,
\qquad L_c^2p_n=\ell^2n(n+2),
\]
and write
\[
K_n=p_nC_n,
\qquad
C_n=c_s^2-\chi\left[1-\ell^2n(n+2)\right].
\]
The physical scalar spectrum begins at \(n=2\). Selective softening of \(n=2\) with stiffening of every propagating \(n\ge3\) requires
\[
1-8\ell^2>0,
\qquad
1-15\ell^2<0,
\]
so
\[
\boxed{\frac1{15}<\ell^2<\frac18}.
\]
Then
\[
C_2<c_s^2,
\qquad
C_n>c_s^2\quad(n\ge3).
\]
The formal \(n=1\) coefficient also lies on the soft side, but \(n=1\) is not a propagating scalar degree of freedom in the regular one-field closed-FLRW sector.

If \(0<C_2\ll1\),
\[
T_2\simeq\frac{S_2}{p_2C_2}
\]
can be enhanced while higher physical harmonics remain regular. For the next physical level,
\[
\frac{T_3}{T_2}=\frac{S_3}{S_2}\frac{8C_2}{15C_3},
\]
so
\[
\boxed{
\left|\frac{T_3/S_3}{T_2/S_2}\right|
=\frac{8C_2}{15C_3}<\frac8{15}.
}
\]
For the pure-dipole \(A_{4i}\) subspace of the selected \(n=2\) harmonic, the observer-sky pattern is exactly dipolar with geometric radial envelope \(\sin2\psi\).
