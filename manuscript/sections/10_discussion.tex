\section{Discussion and open problems}
\subsection{Derived identities and conditional closure}
The novelty claimed is the integrated and audited cosmological construction,
not constrained-perturbation mathematics. The regular exclusion of $n=1$
is inherited geometry. The BHSM proposal is that the surviving response
and couplings are matrix elements of a common retained geometric action.
Writing a projector alone does not establish that ownership.

The degree-two scalar eigenspace on $S^3$ has dimension nine; its dipole
subspace has three orientation components. A two-dimensional temporal phase
space follows after selecting one normalized profile and fixing its axis.
The physical selection and population mechanism remain open.
The R1 coordinates $(\zeta,\zeta_{,N})$ are response coordinates; numerical
identification with canonical BHSM $(q_2,\Pi_2)$ requires a normalization
map and consistent transformation of all observable rows.

The propagator uses gravity--scalar reduced coefficients. Dust is transported
on that response with zero induced matter response at the anchor. This
is not a fully backreacting matter+radiation stability theorem.
The curved effective DAE remains an audit with a documented
constraint-preservation failure.

The positive $p^2$ term in a long-wavelength derivative expansion cannot
justify extrapolation to arbitrarily high $n$. The selected filter window
and near-standard high-$n$ growth belong to the stated reference realization;
the exact parent complement and ultraviolet continuation need separate checks.

\subsection{What the current data establish}
The retrospective SN comparisons leave a mixed result: the conditional
Pantheon+ preference is weak, its transferred correction does not improve
DES, and aggregate tomography does not localize correlations to shared
foreground cells. Fixed external PV means change the score, but their
missing joint covariance prevents a fully marginalized interpretation.
Neither a coherent topographic detection nor rejection of the conditional
framework follows. The prospective cross-observable gates remain unevaluated.

Cross-survey source matching, selection, intrinsic alignments, velocities,
calibration errors and covariance estimation can mimic extra eigenmodes.
The minimal rank bound requires coherent finite latent amplitudes.
A survey-aware null calibration and power study with independent nuisance
constraints are prerequisites for physical rejection.

\subsection{Open scientific inputs}
Upstream open objects are the normalized compact full-preimage background,
complete action-owned local metric/topographic matrix elements, exact seam
export, rare-event/source statistics and first-principles absolute amplitudes.
Publication before closure would rest on reproducible downstream identities,
numerical response checks and restrictions on future realizations.
It would not establish empirical success of complete BHSM cosmology.

Full matter--radiation backreaction, an independently selected normalized
state direction, complete joint observational covariance and an independent
prospective survey design also remain necessary.

\subsection{Residual scientific boundaries}
Three boundaries remain essential to the interpretation.

First, the current common-state basis is the canonical
$X_2=(q_2,\Pi_2)$.  Earlier reduced-propagator coordinates
$(\zeta,d\zeta/dN)$ are retained only as legacy controls and require an
explicit basis transformation before comparison with canonical state
inference.

Second, the closed-space geometry identifies the physical $n=2$ sector but
does not yet derive the unique microscopic population, normalized profile, or
observer-axis selection of that state from the complete upstream action.
Predictions that condition on one normalized profile or the carried-forward
axis must therefore remain labeled conditional.

Third, propagation of the supernova-inferred state becomes a linear-response
stress test at sufficiently high redshift: the saved action-native analysis
finds large metric-response envelopes by $z\gtrsim1.5$.  Quantitative
high-redshift values from that posterior are therefore not promoted as
precision forecasts outside the audited linear regime.

The frozen R1 principal-symbol calculation does, however, close the separate
linear/quadratic high-$k$ stability question over its audited domain: no ghost
or high-$k$ scalar gradient instability is detected there.  This does not
establish nonlinear-fluid stability, ultraviolet completion, or microscopic
BHSM completion.
