\section{Action-provenance gate for a genuinely BHSM-derived cosmological mode}
\label{sec:bhsm-action-provenance}

The preceding sections construct a mathematically coherent BHSM-native
reduction and observable map.  A separate question is whether the physical
$n=2$ cosmological mode has been selected by the presently retained BHSM
parent action, rather than introduced as a compatible bridge degree of
freedom.  The distinction is necessary because later BHSM audits separate
harmonic intuition from retained-action provenance.

\subsection{Hindsight from the retained BHSM action}

The compact closed-$S^3$ topographic effective theory is useful for harmonic
selection, observer projection and late-time response.  It is not by itself
an action-level completion of the retained BHSM parent theory.  In particular,
the retained stationary reflection-symmetric branch does not supply a
nonzero cap-relative transport momentum.  Consequently the existence of the
physical $n=2$ response must be tied to a sourced dynamical branch and a
complete physical Hessian before it can be called an unconditional BHSM
prediction.

This point does not invalidate the bridge theorems.  It fixes their logical
status: they are conditional theorems of the BHSM-native reduction until the
action-provenance conditions below are discharged.

\subsection{Provenance theorem}

\paragraph{Theorem 5 (retained-action provenance criterion).}
Let the BHSM-native cosmological bridge satisfy Theorems 1--4.  Suppose in
addition that the retained parent action admits a sourced relative-periodic
full-preimage solution with all of the following properties:

\begin{enumerate}
\item a nonzero conserved cap-relative momentum difference;
\item a globally constraint-reduced physical $\ell=2$ inertia;
\item a degree-one self-adjoint physical domain with vanishing Green-form
      leakage at the admissible boundary;
\item a complete gauge-reduced Hessian whose physical $\ell=2$ block is
      positive in the kinetic direction and whose mixed metric/topographic
      block is action owned;
\item a common reduction map that carries the retained parent variables into
      the cosmological $n=2$ response without introducing a duplicate scalar
      degree of freedom.
\end{enumerate}

Then the reduced operator $\mathcal K_2$, the metric response
$\mathcal R_{g2}$, the matter response $\mathcal R_{m2}$ and the calibrated
observable kernels $\mathcal F_{\mathcal O}$ are retained-action BHSM
quantities rather than merely compatible bridge constructions.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Items 1--4 provide the nontrivial dynamical background, physical inertia,
self-adjoint domain and complete mixed second variation required to define the
physical Hessian on the retained action.  Item 5 identifies the bridge
variables with that retained physical mode and excludes double counting.
The Schur/Feshbach reduction therefore acts on an action-selected physical
subspace, so $\mathcal K_2$ is action owned.  The metric-response theorem then
gives $\mathcal R_{g2}$ from the same Hessian; covariant conservation gives
$\mathcal R_{m2}$; and the observation functionals produce
$\mathcal F_{\mathcal O}$.  No additional phenomenological mode selection is
required. \hfill$\square$

\subsection{Fail-closed converse}

If any provenance item in Theorem 5 is not established, the manuscript does
not promote the resulting cosmological amplitudes to unconditional
action-derived BHSM predictions.  Instead they are classified as:

\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\text{BHSM-native conditional bridge predictions}
}
\end{equation}

on the stated background and transfer realization.

This fail-closed rule is stronger than simply requiring numerical agreement.
A successful fit, a stable EFT kernel, or agreement with one cosmological
dataset cannot substitute for action provenance.

\subsection{Observable-transport precedent}

Earlier BHSM work constructed a common observable transport functor for a
different physical sector using one declared scheme, one common scale,
explicit threshold conventions and one calibration.  That construction is
used here as a methodological precedent: one common transport map and one
declared calibration are allowed, hidden retuning is not.

The cosmological counterpart is the operator chain
\begin{equation}
\mathcal K_2
\longrightarrow
\mathcal R_{g2}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal R_{m2}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal F_{\mathcal O},
\end{equation}
with the single calibration of Sec.~\ref{sec:bhsm-single-calibration}.

\subsection{Scientific closure statement}

The manuscript therefore separates two levels of closure:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Bridge closure}: Theorems 1--4 define the physical $n=2$ mode,
      induced metric and matter response, observable transfer, common axis
      and single-calibration prediction structure.
\item \emph{Retained-action closure}: Theorem 5 additionally requires the
      sourced full-preimage branch, physical $\ell=2$ inertia, self-adjoint
      domain and complete Hessian to be action selected.
\end{enumerate}

The paper is scientifically complete as a conditional bridge theory once
the numerical kernels are evaluated and the claim labels remain explicit.
It becomes an unconditional retained-action BHSM cosmology only when the
provenance criterion of Theorem 5 is discharged.
