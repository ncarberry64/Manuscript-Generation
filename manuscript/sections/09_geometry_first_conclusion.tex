\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:geometry-first-conclusion}
The geometry-first bridge connects constraints and the physical Hessian to
a selected $n=2$ response, induced metric/matter kernels, fixed-observed-redshift
luminosity distance and optical means and covariances. Its reference-branch
and linear-response assumptions remain explicit. Microscopic source
normalization is not closed, and no general objection to EFT/QFT follows.

Two prospective tests put the corresponding realizations at risk:
one common two-component deterministic state and at most one coherent
stochastic texture amplitude. Rejection at $p<0.01$ eliminates the relevant
realization without extra amplitudes, states, channels or bin selection
after inspection. The failed homogeneous and foreground candidates already
illustrate this discipline. Action-owned inputs and a frozen survey-specific
design remain necessary scientific work.

The frozen supernova sightline test adds a conditional observational check:
weak held-out Pantheon preference does not transfer to DES, and aggregate
cell-sharing tests do not localize a foreground cause. Independent PV means
change the interpretation without licensing a new predictive amplitude.
Complete joint source/environment error propagation remains necessary;
a coherent topographic residual is not established.
