\section{Hyperspherical Parent and Harmonic Convention}

We use a closed FLRW geometry
\[
ds^2=-dt^2+a^2(t)R_H^2\left[d\psi^2+\sin^2\psi\,d\Omega_2^2\right],
\]
with \(a_0=1\). Define
\[
D_C(z)=c\int_0^z\frac{dz'}{H(z')},
\qquad
\psi(z)=\frac{D_C(z)}{R_H}.
\]
Scalar harmonics satisfy
\[
-\nabla_\gamma^2Y_n=\frac{n(n+2)}{R_H^2}Y_n,
\qquad n=0,1,2,\ldots,
\]
so the physical spatial eigenvalue is
\[
p_n(a)=\frac{n(n+2)}{a^2R_H^2}.
\]
A common closed-universe convention uses \(k^2=N^2-1\), hence
\[
\boxed{N=n+1}.
\]

\subsection{Exceptional first nonconstant harmonic}
For our \(n=1\), \(k^2=3\) and \(N=2\). The degree-one scalar harmonics on the unit three-sphere obey
\[
D_iD_jQ^{(1)}=-\gamma_{ij}Q^{(1)}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\left(D_iD_j+\frac{k^2}{3}\gamma_{ij}\right)Q^{(1)}=0.
\]
The scalar-derived trace-free metric harmonic is absent. For the regular generally covariant one-scalar sector, the remaining scalar gauge transformations and lapse/shift constraints leave no ordinary propagating scalar degree of freedom in this exceptional sector. Modern gauge-invariant closed-universe analyses likewise take the propagating scalar spectrum to begin at alternative-convention \(N\ge3\) \citep{KieferVardanyan2022,SpecognaEtAl2026}.

Accordingly, the minimal bridge no longer uses our \(n=1\) as the physical scalar--metric carrier. An additional boundary degree of freedom could evade this conclusion, but it is not required for the construction below.

\subsection{First physical scalar harmonic and exact dipole subspace}
The first propagating scalar harmonic is
\[
\boxed{n_\star=2},\qquad \boxed{N_\star=3},
\qquad p_2=\frac{8}{a^2R_H^2}.
\]
Let the unit \(S^3\subset\mathbb R^4\) embedding coordinates be
\[
X^4=\cos\psi,\qquad X^i=\sin\psi\,\hat n^i.
\]
Every real degree-two scalar harmonic can be written
\[
T_2=A_{AB}X^AX^B,
\qquad A_{AB}=A_{BA},\qquad \delta^{AB}A_{AB}=0.
\]
Writing \(c=\cos\psi\), \(s=\sin\psi\), \(a_i=A_{4i}\), and
\[
A_{ij}=S_{ij}-\frac{A_{44}}{3}\delta_{ij},\qquad S_i{}^i=0,
\]
gives the exact observer-shell decomposition
\[
\boxed{
T_2=A_{44}\left(c^2-\frac{s^2}{3}\right)
+2cs\,\mathbf a\!\cdot\!\hat{\mathbf n}
+s^2S_{ij}\hat n^i\hat n^j .
}
\]
These terms are respectively a sky monopole, dipole, and quadrupole. The pure cross-term subspace
\[
A_{44}=0,\qquad S_{ij}=0,\qquad a_i\ne0
\]
therefore gives an exact sky dipole:
\[
\boxed{
T_{2,\mathrm{dip}}=A\sin(2\psi)\,(\hat{\mathbf n}\!\cdot\!\hat{\mathbf p}).
}
\]
Thus the gauge audit shifts the carrier to the first physical compact-space scalar mode while retaining an exact dipolar observer-sky sector.
