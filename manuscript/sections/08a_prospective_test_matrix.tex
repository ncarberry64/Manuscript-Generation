\section{Prospective observational test matrix}
\label{sec:prospective-test-matrix}

The geometry-first workflow becomes scientifically useful only through
prospective tests that couple more than one observable to the same reduced
physical state. Table~\ref{tab:prospective-tests} summarizes the principal
tests without introducing a new fit in this section.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{p{0.18\linewidth} p{0.23\linewidth} p{0.29\linewidth} p{0.21\linewidth}}
\textbf{Observable} & \textbf{Theory object} & \textbf{Prospective signature} & \textbf{Failure mode}\\
\hline
SN / standard candles & $F_{D_L}(z)X_2$ &
Frozen redshift-dependent distance response with common angular structure &
Amplitude or redshift evolution inconsistent with the independently selected state\\[0.5em]

Growth / RSD & $F_{f\sigma_8}(z)X_2$ &
Same physical state propagated into matter growth &
Requires a second independent state not present in the reduction\\[0.5em]

Weak lensing / Weyl & $F_W(z)X_2$ and optical shear &
Cross-observable angular and redshift consistency &
Axis or transfer incompatible with the distance response\\[0.5em]

Photon redshift / convergence / shear & $Y_\gamma$ &
Joint optical-state covariance generated by common geometry &
Observed covariance structure incompatible with propagated optical state\\[0.5em]

Residual covariance & $C_{\rm topo}=M_OC_qM_O^\dagger$ &
$\operatorname{rank}C_{\rm topo}\le m$ &

\end{tabular}
\caption{Prospective tests of the geometry-first cosmological response. The tests constrain a common reduced state and common source architecture rather than fitting each observable independently.}
\label{tab:prospective-tests}
\end{table}

The one-channel rank test has an amplitude-independent null condition,
\begin{equation}
C_{ii}C_{jj}-|C_{ij}|^2=0,
\end{equation}
for every $2\times2$ minor of an isolated rank-one topographic covariance.
Standard astrophysical covariance and measurement noise must be modeled or
subtracted before this condition is assessed.

The numerical normalization of every row in Table~\ref{tab:prospective-tests}
is not claimed closed at present. The purpose of the table is to specify which
pieces of future data must be explained by one common physical response rather
than by separate per-observable fits.
