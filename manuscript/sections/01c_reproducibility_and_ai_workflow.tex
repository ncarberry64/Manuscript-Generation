\section{Reproducibility and computational workflow}
\label{sec:reproducibility-workflow}

The computational results reported in this work are generated from versioned
source code, machine-readable artifacts, frozen prediction manifests where
applicable, and automated regression tests. Candidate mechanisms that failed
their preregistered or held-out checks are retained as negative results rather
than removed from the scientific record.

\subsection{AI-assisted research tools}

OpenAI ChatGPT and OpenAI Codex were used during the research program as
interactive computational tools for symbolic-derivation assistance, software
generation and revision, test orchestration, manuscript structuring, and
interpretation of intermediate numerical results. Model versions varied over
the development period and are recorded in project/session provenance where
available. AI output was not accepted as evidence by itself: mathematical
claims were transferred into explicit derivations, executable code, automated
tests, frozen artifacts, or independently checkable numerical outputs before
being retained. The author reviewed and edited all retained outputs and takes
responsibility for the scientific content of this work.

The use of these tools does not change the claim hierarchy adopted throughout
the paper. Results remain labeled as derived, calibrated/conditional, rejected,
or open according to the underlying mathematical and empirical evidence.
