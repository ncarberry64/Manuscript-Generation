\subsection{Reproducibility and computational workflow}
\label{sec:reproducibility-workflow}
Versioned code, frozen manifests, numerical artifacts and regression tests
separate prediction generation from target comparison. The source package
and repository guide distinguish numerical checks from observational reruns
whose exact historical input files are not bundled.
OpenAI ChatGPT and Codex materially assisted the workflow; their role and
the author's responsibility are disclosed in the back matter.
AI output is not accepted as independent evidence. Rejected candidates
and open constitutive inputs retain their original claim classes.
