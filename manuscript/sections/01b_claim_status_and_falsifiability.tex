responses, failed candidate reductions, and still-open upstream source
normalizations, we state their logical status explicitly.

The distinction is essential to the methodology of this paper. A derived
structural result is not promoted to a complete numerical prediction where an
upstream normalization remains open, and a failed candidate is not repaired by
post-hoc parameter changes.

\subsection{What would falsify the present construction}

The cosmological realization would be placed under direct tension if
prospective data require, after standard covariance and noise treatment,

\begin{enumerate}
\item incompatible preferred angular axes among observables predicted to share
      the same one-mode response;
\item a redshift-transfer function inconsistent with the theory-selected
      physical state once that state is fixed independently;
\item topographic residual covariance of rank exceeding the number of
      physically admitted texture channels;
\item independent response degrees of freedom not supplied by the retained
      physical Hessian; or
\item a source normalization that cannot be obtained from the common retained
      action without introducing new phenomenological parameters.
\end{enumerate}

These are failure conditions, not targets to be refit after observation.
