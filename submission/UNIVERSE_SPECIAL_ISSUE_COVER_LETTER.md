Please consider “Geometry Before Fields: Constraint-Reduced Cosmology and Optical Predictions in the Berger--Hopf Framework” for the special issue “Cosmological Models of the Universe, 2nd Edition.”

The manuscript develops a conditional cosmological response chain from the closed-space constraint quotient to a selected physical n=2 mode, its induced metric and photon-bundle transport. Its exact observer-shell dipole and two prospective cross-observable rejection gates restrict realizations without introducing independent amplitudes for each observable. The mathematical identities are distinguished from calibrated assumptions and unresolved action-normalized inputs.

We retain mixed observational results. The frozen Pantheon+ source/environment comparison has Δχ² = −0.883, DES transfer has +1.321, and a fixed external-PV sensitivity has −4.909 with incomplete joint uncertainty. Aggregate foreground-shell tests do not establish localization. No cosmological or topographic parameter was retuned, and no signal detection is claimed.

This work substantially expands and methodologically reframes the author's unreviewed Preprints.org study 202601.1427. It does not seek to establish unrelated particle-physics claims. The source, executable checks, frozen manifests, derived comparison tables and input provenance accompany the reproducibility package. Third-party observations are acquired from their original releases; a permanent archive has not yet been deposited.

The contribution fits the special issue's cosmological-model and observational-test scope through explicit, falsifiable restrictions and transparent limitations.
