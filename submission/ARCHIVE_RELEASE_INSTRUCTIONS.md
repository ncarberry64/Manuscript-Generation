No public DOI has been created. This is a prepared release, not a claim of public deposit.

1. Review AUTHOR_CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED.md and insert only confirmed declarations into the manuscript. Verify the intended Universe special-issue route with the editor: the supplied edition/editor/deadline could not be independently confirmed on the accessible MDPI pages during this pass. Do not send this cover letter automatically.
2. Check the final source commit and SHA256 manifest. Rebuild the PDF and figures with the README commands after any author edits. Regenerate both archives and their hashes.
3. Create a versioned record in an archival service such as Zenodo. Use the exact manuscript title, Norman P. Carberry as creator, the final Git commit, and the preprint relationship. Upload UNIVERSE_SUBMISSION_REPRODUCIBILITY_PACKAGE.zip and the final PDF/source archive. Describe the conditional results and missing upstream inputs without claiming observational confirmation.
4. Choose a license only for materials the author owns. This prepared bundle grants no new license; copyright is retained. It excludes third-party raw photometry, covariance grids, models and row-level catalog copies whose redistribution terms have not been established. Preserve original licenses when acquiring these separately.
5. Include the input-acquisition URLs and existing input checksums. Do not upload the 1.16 GB local sightline archive without a separate third-party rights review. Its SHA256 is db704faa3bf6f164017af10ed80028f2799b9f7a1dc4854b88c7108a7dfe8988.
6. Preview metadata and contents, reserve a DOI if the service supports it, and obtain author approval before publication. Once actually deposited, replace the manuscript's permanent-archive statement with the real version DOI and rebuild. Never invent a DOI.

The public bundle reproduces the saved-data figures, document and inexpensive checks. A full observational rerun requires the original pinned raw releases and frozen operators. The compact package is deliberately not described as a complete raw-data archive. Acquisition scripts retain dependencies on the earlier BHSM_PV_INTERPRETATION_TESTS inputs; follow their provenance rather than substituting current releases.
